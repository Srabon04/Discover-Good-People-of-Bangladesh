# 🇧🇩 Discover Good People of Bangladesh (ভালো মানুষের খোঁজে)

**Discover Good People of Bangladesh** is a community-driven digital platform dedicated to finding, recording, and celebrating individuals who are making positive contributions to society across Bangladesh.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
* **Community Submissions:** Easy-to-use form for nominating local everyday heroes and impactful initiatives.
* **Live Analytics Dashboard:** Interactive view to explore statistics filtered by District, Profession, and Department.
* **Verified Contributions:** Highlight positive stories with supporting evidence and social proof.

---

## 🛠️ Built With
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
* **Form & Data Collection:** Google Forms / Cloudflare Workers
* **Analytics & Dashboard:** Looker Studio
* **Hosting & Deployment:** Cloudflare Pages
